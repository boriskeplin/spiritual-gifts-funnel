Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Gifted Ministry ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address and name — when you complete the Spiritual Gifts Assessment or make a purchase.
  • Quiz responses — your answers to the assessment questions. Used only to determine your gift type and personalise your experience. Not shared with third parties.
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Stripe or Gumroad. We never store card numbers or banking details on our servers.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, and click events, collected anonymously via Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your purchased digital products via Gumroad.
  • To send your Spiritual Gifts Profile and any guides you purchased.
  • To send devotional emails and ministry resources tailored to your gift type.
  • To measure and improve the effectiveness of our ministry through anonymised analytics.
  • To show relevant ads to people with similar interests via Facebook Custom Audiences (email is hashed before upload — Facebook never receives it in plain text).

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — payment processing. Privacy policy →
  • Gumroad — digital product delivery and subscriptions. Privacy policy →
  • Mailchimp — email list management and delivery. Privacy policy →
  • Facebook / Meta — advertising pixel for conversion tracking and retargeting. Privacy policy →
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised site analytics. Privacy policy →
  • Vercel — website hosting. Privacy policy →

4. Cookies

We use cookies and browser storage for remembering your quiz progress and preferences, anonymised analytics (Google Analytics), and advertising measurement (Facebook Pixel). You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Your Rights

  • Unsubscribe — every email includes an unsubscribe link. Click it at any time, no questions asked.
  • Access or delete your data — email ministry@gifted.church and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Data portability — you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain email addresses and purchase records for up to 3 years to fulfil our obligations and respond to support requests. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

7. Children

This ministry is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify active subscribers of material changes via email.
